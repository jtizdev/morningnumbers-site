Fair questions

Can this touch my Jobber account?
No. The app is registered read-only with Jobber, so their API blocks it from changing anything. You'll see "view" permissions on the consent screen, and you can disconnect it from Jobber whenever you want.
What does it cost?
Nothing right now. If it becomes a proper product later it'll be a flat monthly price, and the free group will hear it from me first. No surprise charges.
Who's behind it?
Omri. I build business software for a living. I kept seeing Jobber owners ask for numbers they couldn't get without digging (projected revenue, cancelled jobs, a KPI view that fits their business) and figured nobody wants one more dashboard to check. The numbers should just show up.
