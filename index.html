Fair questions

What can you see in my Jobber?

Your numbers, nothing else, and read-only. The app is registered read-only with Jobber, so their API blocks it from changing anything. You'll see "view" permissions on the consent screen, and you can disconnect it from your Jobber settings whenever you want.

What does it cost?

Nothing right now. If it becomes a proper product later it'll be one flat monthly price. No tiers, no per-seat pricing, no annual lock-in, and the free group hears it from me first.

Is this another subscription I'll forget about?

It's one email a week. If you stop opening them, reply "stop" and it stops. No cancellation flow, no retention offer.

What if I leave Jobber?

Then this stops being useful and you should quit it. Nothing holds you.

Who's behind this?

One engineer (me, Omri). I build business software for a living, and I kept seeing Jobber owners ask for numbers they could only get by digging. No sales team, no investors. If you reply to the email, it's me reading it.
