Morning Numbers

The 5 numbers to check every Monday if you run on Jobber

Monday morning, before the trucks roll, is the only point in the week where the numbers can still change what you do. By Wednesday you're reacting. Here's the five-minute version, with where each number hides in Jobber.

1. What you invoiced last week

Not what you quoted, not what's scheduled. What actually turned into an invoice. Compare it to the week before, that's the whole trick. One weak week is weather. Two is a pattern.

In Jobber: Insights → Reports → Invoices, filter to last week.

2. Quote win rate

Quotes converted divided by quotes sent. If you sent 10 and won 3, you don't have a marketing problem, you have a pricing or follow-up problem. Most home service businesses live somewhere between 40 and 60 percent. Falling under that for two weeks straight is worth a phone call to the last five people who said no.

In Jobber: Insights → Reports → Quotes. Converted counts as won.

3. Jobs sold but not scheduled

Jobber marks these "action required". It's work someone already said yes to, sitting in limbo. Every one of these is revenue you earned the hard way and then left on the counter. Monday is when you put them on the calendar, because your customer's second choice contractor also works Mondays.

In Jobber: Jobs list, filter by status. The action required ones are the leak.

4. Overdue invoices, by how overdue

The total is less useful than the age. Money in the 0 to 30 bucket mostly shows up on its own. The 31 to 60 bucket is where invoices start dying, and past 90 days you're realistically collecting half, if that. The Monday question is one name: who just moved from the first bucket into the second? Call that person this week, not the one who's owed you since spring.

In Jobber: Insights → Reports → Aged Receivables. Worth finding, most people never open it.

5. What's booked for this week

Look five days ahead. If Thursday and Friday are thin, you still have Monday and Tuesday to fill them, that's the point of checking now. Quiet weeks don't announce themselves, they just arrive.

In Jobber: the Schedule view, or Insights for booked value if you're on a plan that shows it.
